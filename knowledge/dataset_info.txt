Customer Purchase Behavior Dataset

This dataset contains information about customers and their purchasing behavior.

Important features include:

- Customer Age: Age of the customer.
- Gender: Gender category of the customer.
- Product Category: Category of the purchased product.
- Purchase Amount: Amount spent by the customer.
- Discount Applied: Whether a discount was applied.
- Payment Method: Method used for payment.
- Customer Rating: Rating given by the customer.
- Previous Purchases: Number of previous purchases made by the customer.

Potential analysis questions:

1. Which product categories generate the highest purchase amounts?
2. Does customer age affect purchasing behavior?
3. Do discounts influence purchase amounts?
4. Which payment methods are most commonly used?
5. Is there a relationship between customer ratings and purchase behavior?
6. Which customer groups appear to be the most valuable?

The purpose of the analysis is to identify customer purchasing patterns,
important factors affecting purchases, and useful business recommendations.
